## FAQs
- **Heading:** FAQs — **CTA:** Read all
- **Q: Do I need to meditate?** "Only if you're curious. Meditation isn't about sitting
  perfectly still or emptying your mind. It's more like finally getting to hear your own
  thoughts without everyone else talking over them. And honestly, what you discover can be
  surprisingly fun…."
- **Q: Where do I begin with my inner journey?** "Exactly where you are. You don't need a
  morning routine or a silent retreat in the mountains. You just need one honest moment of
  wondering, 'What's really going on with me?' That's enough…"
- **Q: Is this same as therapy?** "Not quite. Think of this as an invitation to understand
  yourself through holistic practices that have supported human well-being for centuries. We
  slow down, listen inward, and make space for what wants to be seen. Therapy and this work can
  beautifully complement one another…"
- **Q: How do online sessions work?** "We meet over video, settle in together and explore
  whatever feels present through conversation, meditation, journaling or reflection. Wear
  something comfortable, bring a cup of tea if you like and let the rest unfold naturally."
- **Note (voice guidance):** "These feel more human, and more like you're inviting someone into
  a conversation rather than explaining a service."

## Voice & tone summary (for reuse)
- **Second person, inclusive "we/us"** — collaborative, not authoritative.
- **Short sentences, gentle rhythm**, frequent ellipses (…) that trail off invitingly.
- **Reassuring & low-pressure** ("only if you're curious", "exactly where you are").
- **Warm sign-offs** and human microcopy over feature/benefit sales language.
- **Explicit inclusivity + care disclaimer** in the footer (a trust signal worth keeping).
