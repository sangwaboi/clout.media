# 01 — Brand Overview & Design Language

## The brand
- **Name:** What's Within (`whatswithin.space`)
- **Person:** Komal — a Holistic Meditation Practitioner (meditation, breathwork, nervous
  system regulation, shadow work, women's circles).
- **Category:** Wellness / spirituality / personal-growth practitioner site.
- **Tagline in use:** *"The deepest transformation often begins with a simple question:
  What's within?"*
- **Mission (verbatim):** *"What's within's mission is to create spaces where people
  reconnect with their inner world, understand their emotional patterns and build a steady
  relationship with themselves rooted in awareness and presence."*

## Design personality (the mood board in words)
The site's voice and visuals both aim at the same feeling: **warm, human, calm-but-playful,
and quietly confident.** It is emphatically *not* clinical or corporate. Key adjectives:

- **Warm & earthy** — a cream "paper" base and sun-baked reds/oranges.
- **Playful & whimsical** — chunky rounded display type, asterisks, hearts, floral blobs.
- **Feminine & soft** — pinks, rounded everything, gentle copy.
- **Retro-modern** — 70s poster warmth meets clean contemporary flat design.
- **Editorial** — big type, confident color-blocking, lots of breathing room.
- **Approachable & safe** — inclusive language, conversational microcopy, no jargon walls.

## Core design principles (extracted from how the site behaves)
1. **Color-blocking over gradients.** Each section is one flat, saturated background color.
   Depth comes from color contrast and shape, never from shadows or gradients.
2. **One loud voice per screen.** A single oversized display headline anchors each section;
   everything else is calm supporting text.
3. **Cream is the connective tissue.** `#faefe6` recurs as text-on-color, as button fills,
   and as its own neutral section — it stitches the loud colors together.
4. **Rounded, never sharp.** Type is rounded, buttons are full pills, decorations are
   organic blobs. There are essentially no hard 90° corners in the brand furniture.
5. **Human, hand-made touches.** Quatrefoil flowers, hearts, asterisks and blob shapes act
   like stickers/doodles, keeping it personal rather than templated.
6. **Generous whitespace + big type.** Confident scale contrast (200px hero vs 16px body).

## Overall aesthetic reference points
- 1970s wellness / naturopathy poster warmth
- Modern "friendly wellness" brands (rounded sans + pastel color-blocks)
- Sticker-book / collage decoration language
- Editorial magazine cover typography

## Emotional target
A visitor should feel *invited in and put at ease* — like a warm, unhurried conversation,
not a sales funnel. The design deliberately trades slickness for warmth.

## What to carry into a CloutSync revamp (design DNA, translatable)
Even though CloutSync is a different domain, the *transferable* pieces of this system are:
- Flat, confident **color-blocked sections** with one hero color + a cream neutral.
- A **single expressive display typeface** paired with one clean geometric sans for body.
- **Full-pill buttons** with underlined/bold labels.
- **Organic decorative accents** used sparingly as personality markers.
- **Oversized headline scale** with calm, well-spaced body copy.
